# Changelog

## 0.4 — the delivered build

- Five acts, 24 stops from Chumphon to Narathiwat, each with dialogue, a local
  host, one Thai word, culture cards and one Python puzzle.
- 40 heritage landmarks, 25 side quests, 74 road encounters, 18 lore documents.
- Two film-style cutscenes, an eight-tab journal, a sailing map, save/load,
  key rebinding, options, mouse support, a village-naming scene, a fishing
  mini-system and per-region weather.
- Every picture generated in code: the build ships no image files.
- 201 automated test cases that run headless in about 30 seconds, plus
  `tools/check_validator.py`, which proves that all 24 prepared solutions are
  accepted and all 24 pre-filled starters are rejected.

## 0.3

- The story was deepened to more than 21,000 words of dialogue, story and lore.
- Ruined villages were replaced with clustered villages, and the countryside
  was made wilder.
